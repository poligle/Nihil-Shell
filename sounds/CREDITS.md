# Where the sounds come from

- `nihil/`, the pack NIHIL uses: made by [`tools/sounds.py`](../tools/sounds.py), MIT licensed
  as the rest of the code. Running it again makes the same files.
- The previous pack, here, for `soundPack = "classic"`:
  - `menu.ogg`: [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds), Interface Sounds,
    CC0 1.0.
  - `switch.ogg`: made in LMMS.
  - `close.ogg`, `fullscreen.ogg`, `lock.ogg`, `open.ogg`, `power.ogg` and `special.ogg`:
    their source is not recorded.
