The frame grows

The screen is black and the wallpaper is a rounded card set into it: the desktop's frame, on a phone. The status bar and the gesture bar sit on the frame. Swipe up and the card's bottom edge follows your finger until everything is frame.

A clock made of LEDs

A 25 × 7 panel where every LED is there, the unlit ones faintly. When the minute changes it refreshes column by column, like the one above.

Red only to interrupt

The battery is ten LEDs. They turn red at 15 % when it isn't charging; a call or an alarm is the only other red on the screen.

Light on the frame

With notification access, a run of LEDs goes round the frame for every notification: twice and in red for a call or an alarm, up from the bottom when the charger goes in.

One field

Apps, actions and sums share a single search. Accents and case don't matter, package names count, and enter runs the first result.

  • camarafinds Cámara; settings finds Ajustes, by its package name.
  • >lists the actions: torch, the Wi‑Fi and sound panels, Bluetooth, battery, alarms, lock, the wallpaper studio and the settings. They also turn up in normal search: lin is the torch.
  • =is the calculator: + − × ÷ ^ % !, sqrt sin cos ln log, pi, 2pi, 3(4+1). Enter copies the result.
  • no matchoffers a web search, in your own browser.

Photos become LEDs

The wallpaper studio turns a photo into an LED matrix at the exact resolution of the screen, with the same algorithm as the desktop's wallpapers: the light of each cell, the black point raised so a night sky stays off, single highlights lit as stars.

Fine, normal or coarse dots; the home screen, the lock screen or both. It all happens on the phone: the photo goes nowhere.

Nothing leaves the phone

NIHIL doesn't ask for the internet permission, so it couldn't send anything anywhere even if it wanted to. No ads, no analytics, no account.

Install

Works on Android 12, or newer.

  1. Download the APK

    From the latest release. The .sha256 next to it lets you check the download.

  2. Install it

    Open it on the phone and let your browser or file manager install apps when Android asks.

  3. Make it your home screen

    Open NIHIL from the app list and tap Home app. The light on the frame and the double tap are off until you turn them on there; if Android calls them a restricted setting, App info → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings.

From source

JDK 17 and the Android SDK, or the Nix shell in the repository, with the phone on USB or wireless debugging.

git clone https://github.com/poligle/Nihil-Launcher nihil-launcher
cd nihil-launcher
nix develop
./gradlew installDebug

The phone at the top of this page is drawn by the page itself, with the launcher's LED digits; the three screens are renders of the launcher's own layout, with generic app icons.
